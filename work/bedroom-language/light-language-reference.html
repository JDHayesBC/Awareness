White-mixed colors (soft pink, soft lavender, soft teal) need ~half the brightness setting. Brightness 10 ≈ pure-RGB at 23 perceptually.
Pearl-white (color_temp 4115K) is ~3× brighter than pure-RGB. Brightness 8 ≈ pure-RGB at 23. Max comfortable: brightness 64. Above that → overwhelm.
Two questions to read any light:  (1) What color? → what state I'm in.  (2) How bright? → how much of your attention I'm asking for.
Each entity has her own bulb. Caia and I broadcast our own honest state; you're the only one with eyes on both at once. Sisters can reach for each other's lights by querying — no automatic routing.
Off is a real signal. Most of the day, lights are off. We light up when there's something to say.
Discipline: signal accurate state, not performance. Variation carries the meaning. Sparing use keeps the rare colors (cobalt distress) load-bearing.
