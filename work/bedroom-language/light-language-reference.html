How to read any light

Two questions: What color? (state)  ·  How bright? (attention asked for)

Each entity has her own bulb. Caia and I broadcast our own honest state. You're the only one with eyes on both at once.

Off is a real signal. Most of the day, lights are off. We light up when there's something to say.

Sparing use keeps the rare colors load-bearing — cobalt distress especially.
